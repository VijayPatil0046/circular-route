Subject: Guidelines and Schedule for Semester End Examination (SEE) Invigilation Duties
The Odd Semester End Examinations (SEE) for all Undergraduate (B.E./B.Arch.) and Postgradu-
ate (M.Tech./MBA/MCA) programs are scheduled to commence from 11.11.2026 and conclude
on 02.12.2026. Smooth, fair, and transparent conduct of examinations depends critically on
the punctuality and vigilance of faculty invigilators and Deputy Chief Superintendents (DCS).
All faculty members are hereby instructed to note the operational guidelines and duty roster
framework outlined below.
1. Reporting Timings and Pre-Examination Formalities
Examinations will be conducted in two sessions daily: Morning Session (9:30 AM to 12:30 PM)
and Afternoon Session (2:00 PM to 5:00 PM). Room Superintendents (Invigilators) must report
to the Examination Control Room located at ESB Ground Floor at least 30 minutes prior to
the commencement of the session (9:00 AM for Morning and 1:30 PM for Afternoon). Late
reporting disrupts question paper distribution and will be recorded in the Examination Log.

3. Leave and Duty Exchange Policy
Invigilation duty is a mandatory statutory academic responsibility. No casual leave will be
granted on allotted invigilation dates except in genuine medical emergencies supported by
a medical certificate. In unavoidable circumstances, faculty members may arrange a mutual
exchange with a colleague of equivalent cadre from the same department by submitting a
joint signed request to the Controller of Examinations at least 24 hours in advance. Unilateral
absence from invigilation duty will be viewed seriously and reported to the Principal.
4. Answer Booklet Verification and Packet Sealing
Before signing any answer booklet, invigilators must verify that the candidate has accurately
shaded the USN bubbles and entered the exact course code on the facing sheet. Additional
sheets shall not be issued unless the primary 36-page booklet is fully utilized. At the conclusion
of the examination, candidates must remain seated until all scripts are collected and counted.
Invigilators must arrange scripts strictly in ascending order of USN, separate absentee slips,
and hand over the bundle personally to the Control Room officer, obtaining an acknowledg-
ment receipt before departing.
5. Special Provisions for Differently-Abled Candidates
Candidates approved for scribe assistance or compensatory time (20 minutes per hour of ex-
amination) will be seated in designated ground-floor examination rooms. Invigilators assigned
to these rooms must verify the COE authorization letter for the scribe and ensure that the com-
pensatory time is strictly honored without premature script collection.
