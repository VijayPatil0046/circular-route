Ramaiah Institute of Technology has issued guidelines for the Odd Semester End Examinations scheduled from 11.11.2026 to 02.12.2026, requiring faculty invigilators to report to the ESB Ground Floor 30 minutes before the morning (9:30 AM) and afternoon (2:00 PM) sessions. The circular mandates strict adherence to pre-examination protocols, including candidate verification and script collection, while prohibiting electronic devices and outlining procedures for handling malpractice.
