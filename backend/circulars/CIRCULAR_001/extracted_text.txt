Subject: Library Timings Revision during SEE Period
In view of the upcoming Semester End Examinations (SEE), the Central Library timings will
be revised to facilitate extended preparation hours for all undergraduate and postgraduate
students. The revised schedule is as follows:
Revised Timings
: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Monday to Saturday)
Sunday Timings
: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

: 22.10.2026 to 10.11.2026
