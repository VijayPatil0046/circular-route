Subject: NBA Accreditation Readiness Audit and Departmental Course File Verification
In preparation for the upcoming National Board of Accreditation (NBA) Peer Team compliance
review for eligible UG engineering programs, the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) will
conduct a comprehensive Internal Readiness Audit from 18.11.2026 to 24.11.2026. All de-
partments are directed to ensure that course files, laboratory manuals, faculty portfolios, and
attainment registers for the past three academic years (2023–24, 2024–25, and 2025–26) are
thoroughly updated and indexed.

2. Mandatory Contents of Every Course File
Each faculty member must maintain a standardized physical and digital Course File for every
theory and laboratory course taught. The IQAC auditors will verify the following ten mandatory
artifacts:
1. Vision, Mission, PEOs, POs, and PSOs of the Department along with Course Syllabus and
Lesson Plan.
2. Articulation Matrix mapping Course Outcomes (COs) to POs/PSOs with justification notes.
3. Attendance Register and list of slow/advanced learners with documented remedial/enrich-
ment actions.
4. Question papers for CIE-1, CIE-2, Assignment rubrics, and SEE with Bloom’s Taxonomy lev-
els and CO tags.

3. Laboratory and Infrastructure Verification
Laboratory in-charge faculty must display updated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs),
safety charts, equipment calibration certificates, and utilization logbooks in every lab. Non-
functional equipment must be tagged with maintenance work-order numbers. Display boards
outside laboratories should clearly indicate laboratory area, investment value, and mapped
POs.
4. Audit Feedback and Closure of Non-Conformities
Following each departmental audit, the IQAC team will issue a formal Observation Report high-
lighting strengths and Non-Conformities (NCs). Departments must submit a time-bound Cor-
rective Action Plan within seven working days of receiving the report. Full cooperation from all
faculty and technical staff is solicited to uphold the institute’s accreditation standards.
