The Centre for Teaching and Learning at Ramaiah Institute of Technology is organizing a five-day AICTE-recognized Faculty Development Program on Generative AI Tools and Outcome-Based Assessment in Engineering Education from November 16 to 20, 2026, at the ESB Seminar Hall-1. Faculty members are required to nominate themselves through their Heads of Departments by November 5, 2026, to participate in this complimentary workshop, which grants On-Duty status and issues certificates contingent upon 80% attendance and completion of a capstone assessment.
