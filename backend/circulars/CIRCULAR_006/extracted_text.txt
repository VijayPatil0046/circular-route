The Centre for Teaching and Learning (CTL), Ramaiah Institute of Technology, is organizing a
five-day AICTE-recognized Faculty Development Program on “Generative AI Tools and Outcome-
Based Assessment in Engineering Education” from 16.11.2026 to 20.11.2026 at the ESB Seminar
Hall-1.
All Assistant Professors, Associate Professors, and Professors are encouraged to nominate
themselves through their respective Heads of Departments on or before 05.11.2026. Regis-
tration is complimentary for RIT faculty members, and an On-Duty (OD) facility will be granted
for the duration of the workshop. Certificates of completion will be issued only to participants
maintaining a minimum of 80% attendance and completing the capstone assessment.
