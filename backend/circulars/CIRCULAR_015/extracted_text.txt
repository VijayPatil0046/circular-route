Subject: Annual Laboratory Equipment Calibration, Safety Compliance, and Stock Verifi-
cation
To ensure measurement accuracy, workplace safety, and transparent asset management across
all academic and research laboratories, the Institute Asset Management & Safety Committee
hereby initiates the Annual Laboratory Stock Verification and Equipment Calibration Drive for
the financial year 2026–27. All Laboratory In-Charge faculty members and technical instructors
must complete the verification procedures between 16.11.2026 and 30.11.2026.
1. Scope of Physical Stock Verification
Stock verification shall cover all capital assets, precision instruments, computing workstations,
licensed software dongles, and consumable chemical/electronic inventories procured up to
31.10.2026. Each asset must be physically inspected against the Central Asset Register (CAR)
and verified for intact RFID/barcode tagging.

2. Calibration of Testing and Measuring Equipment
Precision instruments in Civil (Universal Testing Machines, Compression Testing Machines),
Mechanical (CNC, Coordinate Measuring Machines, Dynamometers), ECE/EEE (Oscilloscopes,
Spectrum Analyzers, High-Voltage Kits) must carry valid NABL-accredited calibration stickers.
Equipment whose calibration expires before March 2027 must be listed in the consolidated
calibration requisition and forwarded to the Central Purchase Officer by 25.11.2026.
3. Laboratory Safety and Statutory Compliance
During the audit, the Safety Sub-Committee will inspect every laboratory for the following
mandatory safeguards:
• Functional ABC/CO2 fire extinguishers with pressure gauges in the green zone and valid
refill tags.

4. Condemnation and E-Waste Disposal
Obsolete computers, irreparable oscilloscopes, and damaged furniture cluttering laboratory
corridors must be segregated and documented in Form CW-02. Upon approval by the Condem-
nation Committee, electronic waste shall be disposed of exclusively through KSPCB-authorized
e-waste recyclers in accordance with statutory environmental norms.

Inter-departmental verification officers will co-sign the physical stock registers alongside the
respective Lab In-Charges. Consolidated department reports must reach the Registrar’s office
