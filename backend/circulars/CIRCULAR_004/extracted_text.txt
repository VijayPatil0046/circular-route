CIRCULAR
Subject: Mandatory Wearing of Identity Cards inside Campus Premises
It has been observed that several students and a few staff members are entering the cam-
pus and academic blocks without visibly wearing their institutional Identity Cards. In order to
strengthen campus security and maintain discipline during the examination season, all stu-
dents, faculty, and non-teaching staff are strictly instructed to wear their ID cards at all times
within the campus premises.
Security personnel at the main gate, library entrance, and examination halls have been di-
rected to verify ID cards thoroughly. Students who have lost or damaged their ID cards must
immediately apply for a duplicate card at the Student Section before 29.10.2026. Entry to ex-
amination halls will not be permitted without a valid ID card and signed hall ticket.
