Subject: Industry-Academia Advisory Board Meeting and Curriculum Feedback Process
As part of our continuous curriculum enrichment under the Autonomous 2026 Scheme, all
engineering departments are scheduled to convene their half-yearly Industry-Academia Ad-
visory Board (IAAB) meetings between 23.11.2026 and 04.12.2026. The objective of these
deliberations is to align program electives, laboratory experiments, and skill-enhancement
modules with emerging industry practices and National Education Policy (NEP 2020) multi-
disciplinary frameworks.
1. Structured Agenda for IAAB Deliberations
Heads of Departments and Board of Studies (BoS) Conveners must circulate the draft curricu-
lum dossier to external industry experts and alumni representatives at least ten days ahead of
the meeting. Each IAAB session must formally record recommendations across the following
five agenda items:

2. Stakeholder Feedback Collection Protocol
Prior to the IAAB meeting, departments must synthesize structured feedback collected during
the current semester from four stakeholder groups: graduating students, alumni (3–5 years
post-graduation), recruiting employers, and parents. Feedback responses should be tabulated
quantitatively on a 5-point Likert scale mapped to Program Educational Objectives (PEOs) and
Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs). Qualitative observations regarding communication skills,
system debugging, and project management must be explicitly highlighted.
3. Industry Expert Honorarium and Hospitality
External industry members attending the IAAB meeting in person are entitled to sitting hon-
orarium and local conveyance reimbursement as per approved institutional norms. Virtual
participation via secure video conferencing may be arranged for international or outstation
experts. Departments must submit the list of confirmed invitees to the Registrar’s office five
working days prior to the meeting for hospitality arrangements.
