Subject: Holiday Declaration on Account of Kannada Rajyotsava
It is hereby notified for the information of all faculty members, administrative staff, and stu-
dents that the institute will remain closed on Sunday, 01.11.2026, on account of Kannada Ra-
jyotsava. However, the ceremonial flag hoisting and cultural tribute program will be held at
the Main Quadrangle at 8:30 AM.
All faculty members, staff, and student representatives are cordially invited to attend the flag
hoisting ceremony. Regular academic classes, laboratory sessions, and administrative office
operations will resume as usual from Monday, 02.11.2026, at 8:30 AM. Essential campus secu-
rity, hostel dining, and maintenance services will continue to operate uninterrupted.
