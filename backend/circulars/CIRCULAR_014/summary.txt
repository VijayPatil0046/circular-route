Ramaiah Institute of Technology has revised the Odd Semester 2026–27 academic calendar to accommodate lost instructional hours, shifting the last working day to November 7, 2026, and the commencement of Theory SEE to November 12, 2026. Faculty must submit signed Syllabus Completion Declarations on November 7, 2026, while students with attendance between 75.0% and 84.9% due to documented reasons must file condonation applications by November 9, 2026.
