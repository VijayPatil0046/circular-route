Subject: Academic Calendar Amendments and Makeup Class Schedule for Odd Semester
2026–27
Consequent to two unscheduled public holidays and regional rain-induced campus closures
earlier this semester, the Academic Council Sub-Committee has approved minor amendments
to the Odd Semester 2026–27 Academic Calendar. These revisions ensure that all courses meet
the mandatory minimum instructional contact hours stipulated by VTU and AICTE norms prior
to the Semester End Examinations.
1. Revised Academic Milestones
All faculty members and students are requested to note the revised dates in the table below,
which supersede the corresponding entries in the original calendar issued in August 2026.

2. Conduct of Compensatory Makeup Classes
Saturday, 07.11.2026, is declared a full working day following the Monday timetable. Atten-
dance on this day is compulsory and will be factored into the final attendance percentage cal-
culation. Faculty members who still have a deficit of 1–2 instructional hours for 4-credit core
courses are permitted to schedule extra tutorial sessions between 4:30 PM and 5:30 PM on
05.11.2026 and 06.11.2026 with prior intimation to their respective HODs and class represen-
tatives.
3. Syllabus Coverage Certification
Every course instructor must submit a signed Syllabus Completion Declaration to their Head
of Department on 07.11.2026 confirming that 100% of the prescribed five modules have been
covered. Where self-study topics or flipped-classroom modules were utilized, links to recorded
lectures and study notes uploaded on Moodle must be documented in the declaration.
4. Attendance Condonation Protocol
Students whose cumulative attendance in any course falls between 75.0% and 84.9% solely due
to documented hospitalization, bereavement, or representation in university-level sports/tech-
nical competitions may submit a Condonation Application in the prescribed form to their Proc-
tor by 09.11.2026. Applications without authentic hospital discharge summaries or official OD
letters will be summarily rejected. Students below 75.0% attendance shall not be permitted to
appear for the SEE under any circumstances, in strict compliance with autonomous examina-
