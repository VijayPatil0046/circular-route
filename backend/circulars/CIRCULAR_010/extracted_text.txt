Subject: Voluntary Blood Donation Camp Organized by RIT NSS Unit
The National Service Scheme (NSS) Unit of Ramaiah Institute of Technology, in collaboration
with M.S. Ramaiah Medical College Blood Bank and Indian Red Cross Society, is organizing a
Voluntary Blood Donation Camp on 12.11.2026 from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM at the ESB Foyer.
All eligible students, faculty members, and staff are warmly invited to participate in this noble
humanitarian cause. Medical screening, donor refreshments, and official appreciation certifi-
cates will be provided to all donors. Faculty coordinators are requested to encourage student
participation without disrupting scheduled examination activities.
