The NSS Unit of Ramaiah Institute of Technology, in collaboration with M.S. Ramaiah Medical College Blood Bank and the Indian Red Cross Society, is organizing a Voluntary Blood Donation Camp on 12.11.2026 from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM at the ESB Foyer. All eligible students, faculty, and staff are invited to participate, with medical screening, refreshments, and appreciation certificates provided to donors. Faculty coordinators are requested to encourage student participation without disrupting scheduled examination activities.
