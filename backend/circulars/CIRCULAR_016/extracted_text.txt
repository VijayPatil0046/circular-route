Subject: Call for Research Seed Grant Proposals and Publication Incentives (2026–27)
To foster a vibrant research ecosystem and strengthen high-impact scholarly output, the Of-
fice of Research and Innovation (ORI), Ramaiah Institute of Technology, invites applications
from regular faculty members for the Institutional Research Seed Grant (IRSG) 2026–27 and
announces revised guidelines for Research Publication Incentives.
1. Institutional Research Seed Grant (IRSG) Objectives
The IRSG scheme provides catalytic internal funding of up to Rs. 3,00,000 per project (dura-
tion: 18 months) to assist early-career and mid-career faculty in developing proof-of-concept
prototypes, acquiring preliminary experimental datasets, and preparing competitive propos-
als for external funding agencies such as ANRF, DST, DBT, ISRO, DRDO, and AICTE. Interdis-
ciplinary proposals involving two or more departments will receive priority weightage during
peer evaluation.

3. Proposal Submission and Review Timeline
Interested Principal Investigators (PIs) must submit their IRSG proposals in the standardized
ORI template (maximum 8 pages including budget justification and Gantt chart) via the Re-
search Portal on or before 30.11.2026. Proposals will undergo blind peer review by the Re-
search Advisory Committee (RAC), followed by an oral defense in the second week of December
2026. Sanction letters and first-tranche disbursements will be issued by 05.01.2027.
