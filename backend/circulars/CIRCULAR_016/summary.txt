The Office of Research and Innovation at Ramaiah Institute of Technology invites regular faculty to submit proposals for the Institutional Research Seed Grant (IRSG) 2026–27, offering up to Rs. 3,00,000 for 18-month projects, and announces revised financial incentives for research publications and intellectual property. Proposals must be submitted via the Research Portal by 30.11.2026, followed by blind peer review and oral defenses in the second week of December 2026, with sanction letters and initial disbursements issued by 05.01.2027.
