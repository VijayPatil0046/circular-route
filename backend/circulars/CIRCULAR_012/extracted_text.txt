Subject: Instructions for Final Year Capstone Project Phase-I Evaluation (2026–27)
All Heads of Departments, Project Coordinators, and Final Year (VII Semester) B.E. students
are hereby informed that the Capstone Project Phase-I Evaluation will be conducted across all
engineering departments between 16.11.2026 and 21.11.2026. The evaluation aims to vali-
date problem formulation, literature survey depth, system architecture design, and feasibility
of implementation before students progress to Phase-II in the VIII Semester.
1. Constitution of Project Evaluation Committees (PEC)
Each department shall constitute one or more Project Evaluation Committees comprising a se-
nior faculty member (Professor or Associate Professor) as Chairperson, the respective Project
Guide, and two domain-expert faculty members. Where interdisciplinary projects involve mul-
tiple departments, at least one external-department faculty member from the collaborating
discipline shall be invited to the panel.

2. Mandatory Pre-Submission Documentation
Every project batch (maximum 4 students per team) must submit a spiral-bound Phase-I report
along with a digital PDF upload on the departmental LMS at least 48 hours prior to their sched-
uled presentation slot. The report must include an official Turnitin/DrillBit similarity report duly
attested by the Project Guide confirming a similarity index below 15% (excluding bibliography
and standard equations). Teams failing to meet the plagiarism threshold will be deferred for
resubmission with a 10% grade penalty.
3. Alignment with Program Outcomes (POs) and Rubrics
Project Coordinators must ensure that individual student contributions are assessed sepa-
rately using the standardized OBE Project Rubric. Even within a collaborative team, marks must
reflect each student’s specific technical contribution, defense articulation, and understanding

4. Industry-Sponsored and Patentable Projects
Teams executing industry-sponsored projects must attach a formal authorization letter from
the industry mentor detailing the non-confidential scope of work. Projects demonstrating
novel hardware architectures, algorithms, or sustainable civil/mechanical processes should
be flagged by the PEC Chairperson for preliminary screening by the RIT Intellectual Property
Rights (IPR) Cell for potential patent filing support during Phase-II.
5. Marks Consolidation and Submission
Department Project Coordinators shall compile the rubric sheets, attendance records, and final
consolidated marks in the prescribed ERP format and submit the signed summary to the Dean
(Academics) office by 25.11.2026.
