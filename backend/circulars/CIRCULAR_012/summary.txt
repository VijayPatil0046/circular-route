Ramaiah Institute of Technology will conduct the Final Year Capstone Project Phase-I Evaluation for all engineering departments between November 16 and 21, 2026, to assess problem formulation, literature review, and system design. Students must submit a spiral-bound report and a digital PDF with a similarity index below 15% at least 48 hours prior to their scheduled presentation slot. The evaluation is conducted by departmental committees, with a minimum passing threshold of 25 out of 50 marks required to proceed to Phase-II.
